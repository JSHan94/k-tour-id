## Raw finding

### `R5-D5-001` — S2 — A one-result search becomes a blank Map and retains an unrelated score legend

- Fingerprint: `search-map-filter-result-missing-marker+stale-score-legend@v1`
- Severity basis: reproducible usability, truth, and visual-integrity failure. It is not S1 because List and place detail remain usable, but it directly defeats the map’s core job of locating the chosen food place.
- Locale/viewports: `EN · 390×844` and `EN · 801×1000` independently reproduced.
- Surfaces: first-visit discovery, Seoul List, filtered Map, selected place return.

Reproduction:

1. Open the frozen URL in a fresh session at `390×844` or `801×1000`.
2. Choose `Explore as a guest`, open Seoul, and switch to `List`.
3. Search for `느린마을 양조장`.
4. Observe one result, `느린마을 양조장(잠실새내점)`, with simulated score `59/100`.
5. Either switch directly back to `Map`, or open that result and then choose `Close place`.

Expected:

- The one filtered venue remains represented on the map, or the map moves to a view where it is represented.
- The marker/list selection stays synchronized and the map legend describes the filtered map actually shown.
- This follows execution 03 `FL-001` (search/filter must change visible results), execution 07 §7 (bidirectional map/list selection synchronization), §8 (selected marker remains visible with the sheet), and §18 (`map/list selection mismatch 0`).

Actual:

- The result bar still says `1 sourced food places`, and the map root reports `data-signal-source-count="1"`.
- The settled map reports `data-rendered-signal-count="0"` and visibly contains no result marker.
- The legend remains `12× Places · 82 Simulated score · Highest simulated scores at this zoom`, although the only filtered result is score 59 and no score marker is rendered.
- The same mismatch was observed after closing the selected place at `390×844` and after List → Map at `801×1000`.

Traveler impact:

A traveler can find a restaurant by name, open its honest facts, then lose all spatial confirmation on return to the map. The simultaneous `1` result count, blank map, and unrelated `82` legend make it unclear whether the place is off-screen, filtered out, unavailable, or represented by the legend. This undermines both navigation and confidence in what ONDO’s colored score is describing.

Evidence receipt:

- `D5-M02` and the independent filter-map extension in [`coverage/D5.md`](../coverage/D5.md).
- Live settled attributes at both reproductions: `signal-source-count=1`, `rendered-signal-count=0`.
- Live visible strings: `1 sourced food places`; `12× Places`; `82 Simulated score`; `Highest simulated scores at this zoom`.
- The frozen 46-case pixel registry covers `CITY-LIST`, unfiltered `CITY-LIVE`, and `PLACE-PEEK` separately, but not this non-empty searched List → Map/close-return combination; therefore the no-update pixel gate can pass while this traveler path fails.
