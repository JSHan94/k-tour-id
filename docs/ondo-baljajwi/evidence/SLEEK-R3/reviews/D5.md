## Outcome

The product is unusually honest about its demo boundaries. A newcomer can enter as a guest, distinguish official licence records from simulated ONDO scores, inspect unknown facts, open coordinate-based Google directions, preserve a venue across Account/Age/Payment gates, save and reload into My Korea, submit a device-local contribution, keep payment separate from a visit stamp, and enter Labs only after an explicit experimental-boundary acknowledgement.

It is not clean. Three objective findings remain: an unconfigured Residence route can complete Person status, ordinary English food-intent search returns an empty result in both supported cities despite matching records, and one deep modal sequence can leave the reachable venue-Table empty state plus global navigation inert until reload.

## Verified service invariants

- Guest discovery is immediate; short-term, resident, and local onboarding do not auto-launch verification.
- Seoul and Busan each expose 200 sourced records, and official place records remain distinct from the 40 simulated score previews.
- Place details show the official Korean name/address/source record, an explicitly transliterated navigation label, unknown opening/payment/access facts, and coordinate-based Google Directions.
- Account, Person, Age, and Payment KYC appear as separate JIT checks; cancel preserves the exact task in the observed Account, Person, Age, and Payment flows.
- Save returns to the same Roba place; reload preserves the saved place; My Korea returns to the same venue.
- Local Signal explicitly says note/photo stays in the device demo, preserves the draft across Person cancel, changes only Visit + Contribution, and does not change identity, After19, payment, meetup, stamp, or public heat.
- Checkout repeatedly says no real payment/asset movement occurs. Payment alone keeps stamps at `9/10`; the separate reproducible preview visit moves the stamp to `10/10`.
- After19 is an ONDO-only simulated policy, not an official opening/alcohol/admission/age fact, and Age completion returns to the exact venue.
- Tables, chat, photo, report, and feedback consistently state local/session simulation, no live host/reservation/server/moderation, and no nationality/gender matching.
- Public profile is private by default, each location/language field requires separate consent, Person nationality is not copied, and four activity axes never collapse into a safety score.
- Labs requires acknowledgement and consistently labels signer, assets, bridge, trait, badge, OpenDID/EAS, and AMM as `SIMULATED`, `CONTRACT ONLY`, or `DEFERRED`; no real-asset claim was observed.
