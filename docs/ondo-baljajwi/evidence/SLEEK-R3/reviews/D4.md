## Outcome first

The product is unusually disciplined about truth at the transaction boundary: official place records are separated from simulated scores; unknown venue facts remain unknown; checkout repeatedly says no payment occurred; bridge and merchant evidence are marked `SIMULATED`, `CONTRACT ONLY`, or `DEFERRED`; report/photo behavior is explicitly device/session-local; and After 19 does not claim an official venue restriction. Error, cancel, retry, and return copy generally keeps the original venue or task visible.

It is not clean because five objective content defects remain. The most important is a direct contradiction: checkout says its browser-local visit record is not GPS, QR, merchant, or on-site evidence, while the ID/Profile trust surface promotes the result to `Confirmed visits / 확인된 방문` and calls the histories `specific evidence`. Four further issues affect KO identity comprehension, the semantic usefulness of the dietary setting, public-profile field/control names, and the truth of a Table failure message.
