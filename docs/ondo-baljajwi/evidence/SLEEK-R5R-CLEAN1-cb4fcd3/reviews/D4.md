## Deep-lens result

The complete ledgers are in `coverage/D4.md`. The following independent conclusions control this review:

- **KO/EN copy and state messaging:** 14/14 content families were traced in both languages. Loading, empty, pending, success, error, cancel, locked, and unsupported states are registered and visibly distinguishable. The live and frozen evidence use exact boundary language rather than raw enum leakage.
- **Truth taxonomy:** Official place/licence facts are marked `OFFICIAL_SOURCE`/`LOCALDATA`; canonical unsupported facts resolve to `UNKNOWN`; ONDO scores, tables, visits, contributions, receipts, Labs, and After19 activity are explicitly simulated/contract-only as applicable. `DEFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT_CONFIGURED` are not promoted to availability or success claims.
- **Popularity/open/safety claims:** The visible product correctly avoids open-now, live crowd, popularity, foreigner-friendly, and safety-guarantee claims. `D4-CB4-001` is the exception at metadata entry surfaces. Opening-hours/support/menu facts remain unknown when not supplied.
- **Persistence and privacy:** `ondo.preferences.v3` contains locale, guide state, After19 auto-open preference, saved venue IDs, and sanitized discovery choices. `ondo.session.v3` plus the four named feature session keys hold simulated identity/activity/chat/Labs/visit state in the browser tab. Live “Clear this session” removed all feature-session values and preserved the disclosed local preference scope.
- **Return context:** CTA, gate, ID grammar, TTL, future-skew, exact gate sequence, registered venue/table, and table↔venue coherence are allowlisted before mutation. A live injected malformed CTA/gate/context normalized to `gate:null`, `gateState:"idle"`, and map/nation without placing the injected data in URL or history.
- **Provider boundary:** The identity flow states that no request is sent to an external provider. Nationality from identity is never copied into profile. Profile location/language is self-declared, share-off by default, session-local, and not published or sent.
- **Payment and visit:** Payment KYC and simulated checkout are separate from simulated visit evidence. Checkout does not create a visit; distinct accepted visit records, not payment, advance demo stamps.
- **Chat/photo/report/profile consent:** Chat and report/contribution content are session-local. Selected photo object URLs are not persisted or uploaded and are revoked on replacement/close. Profile fields are private by default and require individual share controls; no private marker entered in the live profile appeared in URL or history.
- **After19:** Activation requires a current 19+ result, Korea time at/after 19:00, auto-open enabled, and no manual-off override. Live expired proof normalized `A19-ON` to `A19-OFF`; a valid `A19-MANUAL-OFF` session did not auto-prompt. Expiry and manual-off messaging have recovery paths.
- **Protected attributes and guarantees:** No nationality or gender matching is used. Identity, visits, contributions, and Tables remain four separate axes; none is presented as a character, expertise, or safety guarantee.
