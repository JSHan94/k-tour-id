## Traveler journey assessment

### What works

- **First use:** the value screen explains Seoul/Busan scope and offers a thumb-reachable `Explore as a guest`; the raw-pointer escape landed directly on the Nation surface without KYC.
- **Directory versus signal:** the City map distinguishes outlined sourced-place clusters from solid simulated scores in geometry, copy, and accessible names. `200 sourced food places` and the List alternative are persistently available.
- **Place continuity:** My Korea → saved venue, Place → Tables empty state → exact Place return, and Place → checkout → payment gate → exact checkout return all preserved the selected Roba context.
- **JIT gates:** account, person, age, and payment explain what they do not prove. Cancel returned to the same Table, Local Signal draft, venue, or checkout. The Local Signal note survived a person-gate cancel verbatim.
- **After19:** ordinary official facts remained visible; venue unlock returned to the exact venue with After19 on, while explicitly denying official hours/admission/age claims.
- **Payment/My/Labs:** checkout kept KRW, OOKRW, payment, and visit/stamp semantics separate. My Korea retained the same saved venue and stamp milestone. Labs required an acknowledgement and repeatedly stated that assets and bridges are simulated.
- **Mobile confidence:** raw pointer use covered Guest escape, city entry, List, place details, Tables, a Table, My scroll, and Labs entry. Required actions remained reachable with one hand.

### Where confidence breaks

The four findings below occur at high-stakes interpretation points: whether the map is live, whether a Table is still joinable, whether the traveler has enough facts to start directions, and whether Table members have been identity-checked.
