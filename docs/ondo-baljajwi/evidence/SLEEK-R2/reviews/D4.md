## State and truth assessment

Loading, empty, pending, success, error, retry, cancellation, locked, unsupported, and deferred states are represented where the canonical matrix requires them. Recovery actions remain available and route context is preserved in the targeted gate and Tables checks. Official versus simulated content is usually labeled; unavailable and unsupported behavior is explicit rather than fabricated. Labs exposes contract boundaries and checkout distinguishes preview artifacts from real purchase completion. These strengths do not close the five user-visible content defects above.

No additional objective issue was filed for CTA wrapping. Across all 270 frozen images and the targeted 360x800 and 1440x1000 live checks, the reviewed primary and recovery actions remained readable and operable without clipping or unintended horizontal overflow.
