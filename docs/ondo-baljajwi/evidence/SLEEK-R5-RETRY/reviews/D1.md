### D1-R5R-DESKTOP-CANVAS-WIDTH-01 · S2 · Desktop and large-document surfaces ignore the frozen width contract

Fingerprint: `responsive-shell|canvas>430@min801|document>700|onboarding>430`

Locales and viewports: EN and KO; reproducible at `768×1024`, `801×1000`, and `1440×1000`.

Affected states/flows: `NATION`, `CITY-LIVE`, `CITY-FILTERED-MAP`, `PLACE-DETAIL`, `ONBOARDING-PERSONAS`, and the other document/modal states that inherit the same shell; directly sampled in `FL-001`, `FL-007`, `FL-008`, `FL-009`, `FL-010`, `FL-011`, `FL-012`, and `FL-016`.

Reproduction:

1. Start with fresh B storage and open the frozen `/ondo-b` route at `801×1000` in English.
2. Inspect the rendered `[data-testid="ondo-canvas"]` in `B-PX-NATION-EN`; it is `x=16, width=769px`, with a horizontal bottom navigation.
3. Open `B-PX-PLACE-DETAIL-EN`; the visible detail surface is `739px` wide.
4. Repeat at `1440×1000`; the canvas is `x=130, width=1180px`, and the place detail/onboarding surfaces are `1178px` wide. The map uses a wide left rail, while document text and onboarding choices stretch across the large canvas.
5. At `768×1024`, the place-detail and onboarding surfaces are each the full `768px` wide.

Expected: the canonical interaction spec fixes the desktop app canvas at `430px` from `min-width: 801px`; the visual system additionally caps full-document reading surfaces at `700px` and JIT/onboarding surfaces at `430px` from `700px` upward. Those constraints should preserve the intended quiet single-question hierarchy, compact reading measure, and centered desktop frame.

Actual: the canvas expands to `769px` at the exact desktop breakpoint and `1180px` at `1440px`; full documents and onboarding expand with it. At `1440px`, descriptive paragraphs become very long measures and onboarding turns into a wide three-column form with a large unused lower field. The implementation is internally responsive, but it is not the frozen responsive/art-direction contract.
